Market Pulse · May 2026
What Was Moving the Market

These notes were written for the May 2026 cycle and are kept as background. For current conditions on a specific lane, ask us.

Fuel & Bunker
Bunker Costs Edge Lower
VLSFO prices at Mediterranean ports dipped to around $605/mt this week, offering slight relief on fuel surcharges. Carriers have yet to reflect this in base rates, but further easing could trigger adjustments heading into June.
Türkiye — Europe
TIR Spot Market Remains Tight
Road capacity on Türkiye–Germany and Türkiye–Benelux corridors stays constrained. Early-season produce volumes from Aegean and Mediterranean regions continue to compete with general cargo for available trucks. Spot premiums of 6–10% persist.
China — Türkiye
Asia-Med Rates Plateau After Surge
Ningbo-Mersin spot rates have stabilised after four consecutive weeks of increases. Pre-summer restocking appears largely complete; rates are expected to hold flat through mid-May before potential softening if blank sailings ease.
Middle East
Mersin–Gulf Volumes Pick Up
Türkiye-Gulf trade lanes are showing stronger bookings this week. Mersin-Jeddah FCL volumes are up on renewed demand from Saudi construction projects, while Istanbul-Dubai air freight is seeing steady inflows of textile and apparel cargo.
